Trim & crop

Quick lengths set the end to start + length. 30s, 1m and 2m are the safe lengths: all three fit every output here, including X's 2:20 limit.

While “Draw / adjust crop” is on, drag on the video to draw a box, drag inside it to move it, or drag an edge/corner to resize. Turn it off to use the video controls again.

Quality / resolution

Only changes the frame size each bitrate gets. Size targets and bitrates stay the same. Affects Discord and 4chan only: X, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts use fixed frames and bitrates, so their estimates do not move with this slider.

Outputs

Fit keeps the whole picture and adds black bars. Fill covers the frame and cuts off the edges. Discord and 4chan always keep your clip's own shape.

    Select a video to begin.

    Encoding can take several minutes and may use substantial memory. Files are made one at a time. Discord and 4chan are encoded in two passes for accurate sizes (about twice as long as one pass); the social outputs are single-pass. Keep this tab open and your phone awake. Very long or high-resolution videos may exceed your device's memory.

    Finished files

    Targets are deliberately set below the stated limits to leave room for container overhead. Actual file size can vary slightly by encoder/container; check the final size before uploading.
