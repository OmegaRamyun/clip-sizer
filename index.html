Clip Sizer

Make upload-friendly copies for Discord and 4chan, plus generic Horizontal, Vertical and Square files that post almost everywhere. Video is processed locally in your browser.

Trim & crop

Quick lengths set the end to start + length. 2m is the fits-everywhere length: a Generic file of 2:00 or less posts to X, Shorts, Reels and TikTok.

While “Draw / adjust crop” is on, drag on the video to draw a box, drag inside it to move it, or drag an edge/corner to resize. Turn it off to use the video controls again.

Quality / resolution

Only changes the frame size each bitrate gets. Size targets and bitrates stay the same. Affects Discord and 4chan only: the Generic outputs use fixed frames and bitrates, so their estimates do not move with this slider.

Outputs

Fit keeps the whole picture and adds black bars. Fill covers the frame and cuts off the edges. Blur keeps the whole picture and fills the bars with a blurred, darkened copy of the clip. Discord and 4chan always keep your clip's own shape.

Faster finishes sooner but looks a little worse at the same file size. File sizes do not change. Applies to the MP4 files (Discord and Generic); the 4chan WebM is not affected.

    Encoding can take several minutes and may use substantial memory. Files are made one at a time. Discord and 4chan are encoded in two passes for accurate sizes (about twice as long as one pass); the Generic outputs are single-pass. Keep this tab open and your phone awake. Very long or high-resolution videos may exceed your device's memory.

    Finished files

    Targets are deliberately set below the stated limits to leave room for container overhead. Actual file size can vary slightly by encoder/container; check the final size before uploading.
